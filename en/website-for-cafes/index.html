COMMON QUESTIONS

Before starting a project.

Can the page include a menu, prices and opening hours?

Yes. Information about products and services, prices, photos, contact details and opening hours are included in the one-page website offer.

How much does a café website cost?

Website creation costs RUB 10,000. The first month of maintenance is free. From month two, monthly maintenance is RUB 5,000, RUB 7,500 or RUB 10,000 depending on the selected plan.

Can the website be updated after launch?

The basic content-support plan includes one scheduled update per month; the active-support plan includes three. Additional agreed tasks under the first two plans cost RUB 2,000. New pages, complex functionality and major redesigns are quoted separately.
