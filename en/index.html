FAQQuestions & answers

The essentials, clearly.

Pricing, updates and how to start — without hidden terms.

How much does a website cost?

Design and launch of a one-page website costs RUB 10,000. The first maintenance month is free. Monthly support options and scope are listed above; additional costs are agreed before work begins.

What is included in the Turnkey Website plan?

A one-page website adapted for smartphones, with information about the business, products and services, prices, photos, contact details, opening hours, a map and social or messenger links. Website availability and key links are monitored.

How do the maintenance plans differ?

The Basic plan includes one scheduled website update per month. Active Support includes three. Additional tasks, new pages, complex features and major redesigns are discussed and quoted separately.

Which costs are billed separately?

Domain fees, paid services and other possible expenses are agreed before work begins. Tasks beyond the included scope are charged at RUB 2,000 per agreed task.

How do I start a project?

Send a message on Telegram or MAX, or email us. We will discuss your project, the scope of work and the next step.

Can we discuss a project remotely?

Yes. Projects can be discussed online. Prices on this site are listed in Russian rubles (RUB); scope and any additional costs are agreed individually before work starts.
