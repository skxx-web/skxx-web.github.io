What a business website can include

The one-page website format can present your company, products and services, prices, photographs, opening hours and contact information. Depending on the project, it can also include a map and links to social networks and messaging apps.

  • Custom visual design for the business and its audience.
  • Responsive layouts for phones, tablets and desktop screens.
  • Company information, service or product descriptions, prices and photos.
  • Contact details, opening hours, map and external messaging links.
  • Publication and agreed ongoing content updates.
