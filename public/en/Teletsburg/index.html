PORTFOLIO / REAL PROJECT

Teletsburg restaurant website

A responsive one-page website for a burger restaurant in Saint Petersburg, Russia. The page brings together the project’s visual presentation, essential venue information and clear routes to the restaurant’s current contact details.

Visit the live project ↗

THE BRIEF

Present the venue clearly on one page.

The page helps visitors understand the restaurant, view its visual identity, find the location and opening hours, and continue to the restaurant’s separate live website. This is a web-design portfolio case study, not the restaurant’s official website.

THE APPROACH

What the project includes

01 / VISUAL

Visual direction

Product photography and bold accents support a distinctive visual identity for the burger restaurant.

02 / STRUCTURE

One-page structure

Essential information is organised into a concise page journey and adapted for smartphone screens.

03 / CONTACT

A clear next step

The location, opening hours and contact links help visitors move from discovery to a visit or enquiry.
